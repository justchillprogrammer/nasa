      Every picture here is a real SPHEREx image of this spot, taken months apart. Press Play or Blink: anything that jumps has changed. Or press “Find what changed” and Sky Blink looks for you.

      Pick a spot above to load its images.

      White is brighter than in the reference image, black is fainter. Something that moved shows as a white dot touching a black one. Ordinary stars leave small smudges too, because SPHEREx pixels are coarse, so blink the two images to confirm what you see.

      What changed here?

      Sky Blink can stack every sweep of this view and look for stars that moved or changed brightness. It takes about a minute.

      Colour of the centre point

      Each dot is how bright the centre looked in one image. Every image sees a different infrared wavelength, so together they draw a spectrum. Select a wavelength to view the sky in it.

      Images: NASA SPHEREx Level 2 spectral images, served by the NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive. Brightness values are quick-look measurements, not calibrated science products.
